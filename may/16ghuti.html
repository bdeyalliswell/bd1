How to Play Sholo Guti

  • 1. Setup: Each player commands 16 Guti (soldiers) arranged on their half of the 37-node board, leaving only the center node empty.
  • 2. Step Move: Tap any of your pieces to select it, then tap an emerald-ringed adjacent empty node connected by a carved line to move 1 step.
  • 3. Jump Capture: Leap straight over an adjacent enemy Guti onto an empty node directly behind it along the same line (pulsing crimson/gold rings) to capture it!
  • 4. Multi-Jump Chain: After capturing, if the same piece can immediately capture another enemy, you may continue jumping in a chain combo — or tap End Jump Chain to stop.
  • 5. Victory: Capture all 16 opponent pieces (or leave your opponent with zero legal moves) to win!
